### The problem
Humans face practical obstacles that can separate what they understand or value from what they do: difficulty finding reliable resources, remembering responsibilities, interpreting context, adapting guidance to accessibility needs, or evaluating whether tools help.

The bug is **not** a flaw in scripture, religious practice, or divine governance. The bug is an information-and-support gap in human learning and daily practice.

### Desired improvement
Respectfully request from Allah knowledge, wisdom and opportunity for humanity to develop appropriate assistive technology that can make religious education, remembrance, voluntary good action and environmental stewardship clearer and easier, within each person's own tradition and freedom of conscience.

### Five implementation domains
1. **Faith & Worship:** calendars, opt-in reminders, explanatory materials following the relevant religious tradition. Example: prayer times can follow a disclosed geographical method; the application must not invent new worship rules.
2. **Knowledge & Education:** source libraries, searchable explanations, context and differing interpretations, and accessibility for diverse audiences.
3. **Family & Humanity:** inclusive teaching materials, child-protection information, voluntary giving and welfare guidance; never collect child data without a properly reviewed lawful basis.
4. **Nature & Environment:** educational tools to improve resource use and local environmental care, measured using ordinary science.
5. **Technology & Accountability:** traceable references, transparent limitations, correction workflow, privacy safeguards and practical utility reviews.

### Seven-stage mechanism
Source → Context Check → Purpose Definition → Assistive Product Design → Safety Review → Human Action → Feedback & Correction.

This is a human engineering procedure, NOT a newly revealed religious doctrine or a literal scriptural algorithm.

### Safety and conflict resolution
- Respect believers and non-believers. Use is always voluntary.
- Never turn a personal report or private interpretation into a universal worship rule.
- **QIBLAT UTARA (#006)** is archived as a personal proposal, not a modification of Islamic qibla. Qur'an 2:144 is relevant to the direction of prayer.
- No digital product can decide heaven/hell, spiritual merit, penance, the validity of prayer, or God's judgment.
- Preserve distinctions between source text, an interpreter's reading, personal testimony, and independently verified evidence.
- Do not require user accounts, tracking, or surveillance to demonstrate usefulness.
- Child-protection information must focus on safe real-world assistance.
- Measure educational usefulness and system behavior, not the quality of someone's faith.
